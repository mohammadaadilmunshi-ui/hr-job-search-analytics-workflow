## What the Workflow Does

This project is a human-reviewed HR analytics workflow designed to make the modern job-search process more structured, strategic, and data-informed.

Instead of treating job applications as a simple submit-and-wait process, the workflow turns each opportunity into a clear action plan by combining job tracking, fit analysis, ATS alignment, resume review support, networking research, and next-step planning.

### Core Capabilities

* **Job opportunity intelligence:** Captures and organizes job opportunities in a structured system for easier comparison and follow-up.
* **Role-fit scoring:** Evaluates each role based on relevance, skill alignment, timing, location, and application readiness.
* **Application priority ranking:** Calculates job match score and job landing priority to identify which opportunities deserve the most attention.
* **ATS keyword alignment:** Reviews how well the resume and application materials align with the job description.
* **Tailored resume draft support:** Prepares role-specific resume drafts using verified experience and skills for human review.
* **Cover letter draft support:** Creates targeted cover letter drafts that can be reviewed, edited, and personalized before submission.
* **Networking research:** Identifies relevant networking targets such as recruiters, HR partners, people operations contacts, and early-career recruiters.
* **Contact strategy:** Explains why each contact may be useful based on the company, role, and hiring context.
* **Outreach draft support:** Prepares networking message drafts for review before sending.
* **Action queue:** Builds a prioritized list of next steps, deadlines, resume status, outreach status, and follow-up actions.
* **Daily opportunity summary:** Summarizes top opportunities, pending actions, and application readiness in one place.

### Why This Matters

Today’s job market requires more than submitting the same resume repeatedly. Candidates need to understand role fit, tailor materials carefully, connect with the right people, and stay organized throughout the process.

This workflow was built to support that process with structure, consistency, and human decision-making.

### Human Review and Safety

The workflow does not auto-apply to jobs or auto-send messages. Every resume draft, cover letter, networking message, and application decision requires human review before use.

The goal is not automation for mass applying. The goal is smarter preparation, better organization, and more intentional job-search execution.
